0.2.198
- waypoints - the quest NPC is marked over its nameplate: quest name, Ready for turn-in, chevrons
- waypoints - further out a light beam rises from that nameplate, with the distance under it
- waypoints - distance and travel time to the waypoint ride on the arrow
- waypoints - follows the tracked quest: its hand-in NPC at the nearest spot in your zone
- waypoints - /way [zone] x y [label] in TomTom's syntax, only when no other addon owns /way
- waypoints - with TomTom installed, the waypoint TomTom's arrow follows is shown instead
- options - a Waypoints tab: every part on or off, size, beam colour, Pinpoint distance
- saved data - new keys profile.waypoint and char.waypoint; nothing saved before is changed
