## Description

# PandaQuest

Find your next quest objective, explore your game's world and learn from other players' journeys.
PandaQuest is a standalone quest helper for **Mists of Pandaria Classic, Classic Era,
The Burning Crusade Classic Anniversary and WoW Forever**. One download loads the correct
database for your game.

### In game

- Follow a navigation arrow to quest objectives, turn-ins and available quests, with distance
  and estimated travel time.
- Browse quests, search and filter the database, explore quest chains and view map previews.
- See quest pins on the world map and minimap, with NPC, item and objective tooltips.
- Find gathering nodes with native WoW resource icons, respawn information where measured,
  and advice based on your profession skill.
- Check measured flight times and follow the flight progress bar.
- Read your own profession recipes locally; supported features depend on the game client.
  Archaeology tools are available in MoP Classic.
- Use optional TomTom waypoint integration and QuestieDB gap filling.
- Open PandaQuest with `/pq`, options with `/pq options`, and help with `/pq help`.

### PandaBase — your game's companion website

Visit **[pd.zroot.it](https://pd.zroot.it/)** for quests, NPCs, objects, items, maps,
races, classes and profession information. Choose your game to see its own data.
Community records add quest timings, travel information and gathering discoveries where players
have shared enough evidence. Coverage varies by game; measured results are not invented when
samples are missing. Gear recommendations are currently available for MoP Classic.

WoW Forever uses the live dataset, including data collected during beta. Forever character names
include both parts of the name and are displayed without realms. Public character profiles can
be shared; you control whether your character is public.

### Optional PandaQuest Sync

The separate Sync app discovers WoW installations and synchronizes supported games together.
It uploads recorded gameplay and character snapshots, and downloads community guides into your
already-installed addon. It does not install or update the addon itself: use CurseForge or the
published addon releases for that.

Download the Windows or Linux app and read setup instructions at
**[pd.zroot.it/setup](https://pd.zroot.it/setup)**. Retail and PTR/beta installations may be
recognized, but full addon and website support for those clients is still in progress.

### Sharing is your choice

PandaQuest works without the website or Sync app. At first launch, choose whether to enable
telemetry; recording stays off until you accept. Change your choice later with `/pq consent`
or in Options. While recording is off, no telemetry events are recorded.

If you opt in, recorded data can include quest progress, quest-related kills and loot,
gathering locations, travel, flight times, deaths, level-ups, equipment and character/guild
information. The addon writes data locally and cannot upload anything itself. Uploads require
the separate Sync app. See **[the privacy statement](https://pd.zroot.it/privacy)** for details
and your data controls.
